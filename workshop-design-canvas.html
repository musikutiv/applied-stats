Design the experiment and the analysis together

Compound R workshop · pairs / small groups · 6 minutes
Use keywords, arrows or a sketch. There is more than one defensible design.

Question and primary readout

What exactly do we want to learn? What is measured first — and what is exploratory?

Experimental unit and N

What is independently repeated? How many times, and based on what?

Pairing and allocation

What should share a day or plate? How are vehicle and R assigned to wells?

QC and exclusions

Which controls and rules are fixed before the results are seen?

Analysis

Which effect, on which scale? One value per what?

Report

Which figure? Which interval? What is shown about every experiment?

Discuss: Which change matters most? What information is still missing? What claim would remain unsupported?
