# Final workshop: compound R

## Story

A colleague presents: *"Compound R suppresses inflammation."* LPS-stimulated macrophages were treated with vehicle or compound R (10 µM, 24 h); IL-6 in the supernatant was measured by ELISA. The slide shows a bar chart (mean ± SEM), *** p < 0.001, n = 3, "representative of independent experiments".

The case then unfolds one ordinary mistake at a time, each linked to a key message of the course:

| Slide | What is revealed | Course link |
|---|---|---|
| What was actually repeated? | 4 experiments on 4 days; each day one vehicle and one R culture well; each supernatant in 3 ELISA wells. The figure shows day 1 only; n = 3 counts ELISA wells. | Experimental unit, technical vs biological replication, metadata |
| Day 4 was left out | Dropped after seeing the results; no rule in advance; no unstimulated control to check "stimulation failed". | Exclusions, prespecification |
| Look at all four experiments | Pooled linear bars hide the effect behind five-fold day-to-day variation; paired-by-day log plot shows R lower on days 1–3. | Pairing/blocking, log scale, honest figures |
| Analyse the experiment, not the wells | Per-experiment log fold change: 0.65 (95% CI 0.46–0.92), p = 0.03 without day 4; 0.74 (0.46–1.18), p = 0.14 with it. | Unit of analysis, p vs effect, post-hoc decisions |
| Why only IL-6? | TNF and IL-1β were also measured and showed no consistent change. | Exploration vs confirmation, selective reporting |
| What can these data support? | Narrow claim: R lowered IL-6 in 3 of 4 experiments — worth a planned repeat. | Scope of claims |

Then: small-group redesign (5 prompts; printable `workshop-design-canvas.html`), one defensible repeat (IL-6 primary; ~7 paired experiments for a 30% reduction given SD ≈ 0.4 in log2 fold change; QC rule with an unstimulated well fixed in advance; paired log fold changes with CI), what improved, and "My p-value is below 0.001. Is my result real?"

## Data

All values are fictional, generated by `scripts/generate_workshop.R` (seed 20261005) and validated by `scripts/check_workshop.R`. Key numbers:

| | IL-6 vehicle (pg/mL) | IL-6 R | Fold change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Day 1 (shown) | 834 | 484 | 0.58 |
| Day 2 | 2444 | 1854 | 0.76 |
| Day 3 | 1411 | 871 | 0.62 |
| Day 4 (dropped) | 501 | 558 | 1.11 |

Reported analysis: Welch t-test on the three day-1 ELISA wells per condition, p = 0.00008. This measures ELISA precision on one day, not the effect of R.

## Facilitation

Let participants supply the reasoning; notes contain more than should be said. The researcher is a colleague asking for help, not a culprit — every step in the story is common practice. Protect six minutes for the redesign.
